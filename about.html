Our Story

About Kadavu

Our Beginning

Born from a Love of Hospitality

Kadavu Serviced Villas was founded with a singular vision — to create a haven where guests feel cared for as individuals, not just visitors. The name "Kadavu" draws from a word meaning crossing point — a threshold between the everyday and the extraordinary.

Our founders spent years travelling, studying the finest boutique retreats across the world, before bringing those learnings home to craft something truly their own. Every corner of Kadavu reflects that global sensibility rooted in local warmth.

Our Philosophy

Luxury That Breathes

At Kadavu, luxury isn't about excess — it's about ease. We believe in spaces that feel alive: natural light pouring through wide windows, gentle breezes through open courtyards, and the quiet luxury of unhurried time.

Our team is carefully trained to anticipate your needs while respecting your privacy. Whether it's a candlelit dinner by the pool or an early morning yoga session on your private terrace, we make it happen — effortlessly.
